Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information fishcut stores, where it stores it, and what it uses it for. fishcut is software that runs on your computer, and the operator does not store your videos, tokens or metrics.

Effective 2026-10-27 / Last updated 2026-11-27

This policy describes the running version and its limits. Automatic deletion and Google checks cannot run on a stopped or sleeping computer. Publishing this policy does not mean that Google, Meta or TikTok has approved the app or granted an exception to offline deletion deadlines. See section 8 for operating conditions and section 7 for storage protection.

1. Where this policy applies

This policy applies to fishcut (the desktop app and the command-line tool), an Apache-2.0 open source macOS desktop video editor, and to this website. fishcut is operated by 에이든 (Aiden), a sole proprietorship represented by 안승원 (Seungwon Ahn). Business details are on the operator page.

  • fishcut runs on your computer. Your videos, projects, tokens and metrics are stored on this computer and are not sent to the operator's servers. The only server the operator runs is the auth relay used to connect Instagram and TikTok, described in section 3.
  • When you connect through the Google Cloud project that the operator registered, the Google consent screen shows the app name fishcut, and this policy applies.
  • Instagram and TikTok connections use a Meta app and a TikTok app that the operator registered, or apps you registered yourself and entered in fishcut. The consent screens of those services show the app's name, and this policy applies.
  • When you connect with an OAuth client you created yourself, you manage that Google Cloud project. In that case too, the fishcut software handles information in the way this policy describes.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

fishcut uses YouTube API Services to check the YouTube channel you connected and to upload the videos you have checked. When you use the YouTube features, the Google Privacy Policy also applies. The YouTube Terms of Service are covered in the Terms of Service.

3. Use of Instagram, Facebook and TikTok APIs

Instagram and TikTok connections are optional. If you do not connect these accounts, fishcut does not handle the information below. When you connect one, fishcut posts the videos you have checked to that account, and reads the performance of the videos you posted and of the account to show it on the Metrics screen. When you use these features, the Meta Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy also apply, together with each platform's terms for developers.

TikTok

fishcut uses TikTok for two things: uploading and reading metrics.

  • Upload: it sends the video for which you pressed the confirm button to the connected account through the TikTok Content Posting API. The default is to send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft (video.upload). It posts directly to your profile (video.publish) only if you turned that on separately when you connected, and in that case you choose who can see the video yourself, with no default. You finish an inbox draft in the TikTok app, where you set the caption and who can see it.
  • Reading metrics: the Metrics screen reads and shows only the metrics of the connected TikTok account itself. Through the TikTok Display API it reads the account's follower count, following count, total likes and video count, and each video's views, likes, comments and shares. When the account owner has authorized access through the TikTok API for Business (Accounts API), it also reads the profile metrics and video insights of that same account (views, likes, comments, shares, average watch time, completion rate and traffic sources) through that API. That access starts only after the account owner authorizes it on TikTok's consent screen, and the owner can revoke it on TikTok at any time.

Permissions fishcut asks TikTok for:

  • user.info.basic: to show the name and ID of your account.
  • user.info.stats: to show the follower count, following count, total likes and video count on the Metrics screen.
  • video.list: to read the account's list of videos and each video's numbers for the Metrics screen.
  • video.upload: to send drafts to your inbox.
  • video.publish: requested only if you turn on direct posting when you connect.

fishcut does not read information that needs the user.info.profile permission, such as the profile bio, or TikTok messages or other people's account information.

Instagram and Facebook

  • Upload: it posts the video you have checked as a reel to the Instagram professional account you connect, using the Instagram API with Facebook Login for Business. A reel is public as soon as it is published. Instagram does not accept a direct file transfer for reels longer than about 50 seconds, so only if you connected Google Drive, fishcut uploads the video to your Google Drive (drive.file), shares it so that anyone with the link can view it, and sends that address to Instagram.
  • Reading metrics: it reads the connected account's list of posts with likes, comment counts and follower count, and the insights of reels and of the account (views, reach, average watch time and so on). If you allowed the comment-reading permission, it also reads the text of comments on your posts, and it neither reads nor stores the names of the people who wrote them.
  • Permissions: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_read_engagement and pages_show_list are used to find the Facebook Pages you manage and the Instagram accounts linked to them, and to post reels. instagram_manage_insights and instagram_manage_comments are optional permissions used to read metrics.
  • fishcut does not read your Facebook or Instagram messages or friends. When you register a public business account as a followed account in reference discovery and press refresh, it can read that account's reels (the first line of the caption, posted date, views, likes and comment counts) through the official Instagram API. These values are saved on this computer as your reference candidates, not as metrics of the connected account.

  • Requests to upload videos and to read metrics go directly from your computer to Meta (graph.facebook.com, rupload.facebook.com) and TikTok (open.tiktokapis.com) over HTTPS. Videos and metrics do not pass through the operator's servers.

  • fishcut does not sell the information it reads from TikTok, Instagram or Facebook, and does not share it with third parties. The operator does not receive it, so it is not combined across users, and it is not used for creator discovery, ranking, advertising, profiling, credit assessment, or training AI or machine learning models. Metrics are used only to show them on the Metrics screen to the owner of the connected account.
  • If you have an AI agent read the metrics, the service of that agent's provider may receive what it reads. You decide which agent to use and whether to have it read metrics (section 6). fishcut does not pass tokens to agents.
  • You can revoke TikTok access directly on TikTok. Remove fishcut in TikTok settings, or in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions. Deletion and revocation are described in section 9.

Retention is described in section 8, and deletion and revoking access in section 9.

5. How fishcut uses the information

  • It shows the name of the channel or account you are about to upload to, so you can confirm that it is the right one.
  • It uploads the video for which you pressed the confirm button to the connected channel (YouTube, Instagram or TikTok), with the settings you chose.
  • After a scheduled upload, it checks the video's visibility status and scheduled time again and shows whether they match your request. For an unscheduled upload, it records the upload response as the result.
  • It reads the metrics of the videos you posted and of the connected accounts and shows them on the Metrics screen, so you can see how your videos performed.
  • It lets the AI agents you call through the fishcut CLI and skills read the name of the connected channel and the upload records, so they can help you plan videos and prepare uploads (section 6).

fishcut does not use this information for advertising, profiling, sale, or credit assessment.

6. Sharing and transfer

  • fishcut does not send the information above to the operator's servers. Logins through the auth relay in section 3, requests to Google, Meta and TikTok, and transfers to AI agents and voice providers you connect are described below.
  • YouTube channel checks, uploads and metric reads go directly from your computer to Google over HTTPS. There is no server in between.
  • Instagram and TikTok uploads and metric reads also go directly from your computer to Meta and TikTok. Only the login connection and the refreshing and revoking of TikTok tokens pass through the auth relay in section 3.
  • AI agents that you call through the fishcut CLI and skills (such as Claude Code or Codex) can read part of what is stored on your computer: video projects, channel settings (channel name, planning settings, upload defaults, default voice, publishing schedule, and the name of the connected channel), upload records (channel name, time, visibility or schedule, video address, the title sent, and pinned comment), and the metrics you have the agent read. You decide which agent to connect, and that agent provider's policies also apply. fishcut does not pass tokens to agents. Instagram comment text is not part of the metrics an agent reads, and the CLI on this computer returns it only when you ask for it directly.
  • If you choose a paid voice provider (such as Typecast or ElevenLabs) and approve it, the sentences to be voiced are sent to that provider with your API key. That provider's policies apply to this transfer. It contains no Google user data.

7. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • fishcut creates the state folder with permissions that let only the user who runs fishcut read and write it (0700 for the folder, 0600 for files).
  • Google, TikTok and Instagram tokens and app secrets are stored in the macOS Keychain and are not kept in files in the state folder. When fishcut writes to the Keychain, it passes the value on standard input so that it does not appear on the command line.
  • Channel information, publishing records, metrics, content you wrote and project files are stored as files in the state folder and work folder, and fishcut does not encrypt them separately.
  • All communication with Google, Meta and TikTok is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • By default the local editor and daemon accept connections only at this computer's address (127.0.0.1), and only a window or browser opened with the token issued at startup can use them. If you turn on remote access yourself, that setting applies.

8. Retention period

fishcut stores on this computer the information needed to check connected channels, upload videos you approve, and show the Metrics screen. The automatic processing below runs only while fishcut is running.

A TikTok refresh token stops working when the validity period TikTok sets (up to 365 days after issue) ends.
TikTok and Instagram publishing records, metrics and channel analysisKept until you delete them while the account is connected. When you disconnect, they are gathered separately on this computer and kept for 30 days only, and reconnecting the same account attaches them again. After 30 days they are deleted while fishcut is running. Instagram comment text is deleted immediately on disconnect.
User-written text, channel plans, upload drafts and selections, OAuth client file, project filesKept until you change or delete them. Google revocation or API-record cleanup alone does not delete authored content or project files.
Policy-acceptance revision and timeKept in the state folder and updated when you accept a different revision. Accepting the same revision again does not change its original acceptance time. Deleting the state folder removes it.

Existing connections without a verification timestamp are not assigned a new retention period automatically. Automatic cleanup stops; a separate verification records the actual time only after Google confirms the same channel.

Operating conditions: Google checks and disk deletion cannot run while fishcut is stopped or the computer is powered off or asleep. Retention is checked first on restart. Without a network connection, Google checks and revocation requests cannot complete. Because of these limits, the current local way of running does not guarantee calendar-based deletion deadlines that include offline periods.

9. Deletion and revoking access

  • Disconnecting YouTube or deleting a connected profile in channel management first disables the connection and immediately requests Google token revocation. Other local profiles using the same YouTube channel or token are also disabled. Any upload in progress must first have its completion status confirmed.
  • A failed Google revocation request is not shown as success. The screen shows revocation pending and offers a retry; automatic retries also run while the editor is open. Google revocation and local information deletion are kept separate.
  • Withdrawal deletes related API results, upload session URLs and duplicate-upload fingerprints locally within 7 days. User-written text, plans, drafts and project files are preserved.
  • You can also revoke access on the Google Account permissions page. While running, the editor checks the connection periodically, and if Google explicitly reports the token as invalid, it deletes that connection and its related API records. It does not assume access was revoked just because of a network failure or a wrong setting.
  • Deleting the state folder is a local deletion; it does not send a revocation request to Google or remove the tokens in the Keychain. To remove Google's grant too, use the permissions page above.
  • Local deletion or revoking app access does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube. YouTube manages its own videos and data.
  • Disconnecting a TikTok channel in channel management deletes its tokens from the Keychain, asks TikTok to revoke the token, and stops reading TikTok metrics. That channel's publishing records and metrics are kept separately on this computer for the 30 days in section 8, then deleted. To remove kept data right away, delete the fishcut state folder. To end access on TikTok itself, remove fishcut in TikTok settings, or in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions.
  • Disconnecting an Instagram channel in channel management deletes its tokens from the Keychain and stops reading metrics. fishcut does not ask Meta to revoke the app's permission, so to end the app's access on the service itself, remove it in Facebook's Business Integrations settings (access to the Page and the Instagram account ends).
  • Data deletion instructions for Facebook and Instagram: the operator does not hold a copy of the information that fishcut receives from Meta, so there is nothing to delete on the operator's side. To delete what fishcut holds, disconnect the channel or delete the fishcut state folder as described above, and remove the app in Business Integrations to end its access. Send questions to the address in section 15.
  • Deleting local information does not delete reels already posted to Instagram, or drafts and posts on TikTok. Those are managed by the services.
  • Automatic processing is subject to section 8's operating conditions. fishcut does not guarantee secure erasure of OS backups, snapshots or state-folder copies you made separately.

10. Device storage and cookies

  • The fishcut software creates a state folder (tokens, channels, upload history) and a work folder on your computer.
  • The local editor sets a session cookie so that only the browser opened with the startup token can use the editor. It may keep convenience information, such as display settings, in browser storage. This cookie and this storage are used only for the editor's address on this computer.
  • This website does not use cookies or analytics tools. It loads fonts from outside font services (jsDelivr, Google Fonts) and is served through a hosting provider, so these providers may keep access logs (IP address, browser information).
  • When you press play on a YouTube video among the example shorts on the home page, that card loads the YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is requested from YouTube before you press play. Once the player opens, YouTube may use cookies or browser storage, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to that information.

11. Ads and third-party content

fishcut and this website do not show ads, and they do not allow third parties to place content or ads in them. The YouTube player on this website opens only for an example short that a visitor presses play on, and YouTube decides what appears inside the player.

12. Limited Use of Google user data

When fishcut uses information it receives from Google APIs, or transfers that information to another app, it follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

fishcut's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • fishcut uses Google user data only for the features in section 5 above.
  • The operator does not receive Google user data, so the operator does not read it.
  • fishcut does not use Google user data to train AI or machine learning models, and it does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

13. Children

fishcut is not a service made for children, and it does not knowingly collect information about children. Each time you upload, you set whether the video is made for kids.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the last updated date on this page is changed. The editor shows links to the fishcut Privacy Policy, fishcut Terms of Service, YouTube Terms of Service and Google's permission-revocation page. A person explicitly accepts all three policies and terms by selecting the initially unchecked checkbox and pressing a separate acceptance button. The accepted revision and time are recorded in local publishing state; an agent cannot accept on your behalf.

The in-app acceptance step described here applies to connecting a YouTube channel. Instagram and TikTok connections do not require it; sections 3 and 9 of this page describe how they are handled.

An existing Google connection is not treated as policy acceptance, and a connected channel can accept separately without reconnecting. The server checks the current accepted revision before Google connection and upload review or transmission. Draft editing and saving, disconnection and revocation retries are separate from policy acceptance, but storage-protection errors can still stop operations as described in section 7.

When the app changes the revision it requires, acceptance is required again. Changing only this webpage's last-updated date does not automatically change the app's acceptance revision. New data uses or purposes must be introduced only after the necessary advance notices and consent process are in place.
